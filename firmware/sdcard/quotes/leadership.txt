#Quotes from the leadership book "The Servant"
Listening is one of the most important skills a leader can choose to develop.
Authority is the skill of getting people to willingly do your will because of your personal influence.
You can get a few seasons out of power, but over time, power can be very damaging to relationships.
Leadership is all about meeting needs.
A good leader is: Honest, a good role model, caring, committed
A good leader is: A good listener, holds people accountable, treats people with respect
A good leader: Gives positive encouragement, has an enthusiastic attitude, appreciates people
The key to leadership is accomplishing the tasks at hand while building relationships.
Trust is the basis of all relationships.
The greatest pleasures in life are absolutely free.
A leader is someone who identifies and meets the legitimate needs of their people and removes barriers for them.
To lead, you must serve.
Anyone wanting to be a leader must first be a servant. If you want to lead, you must serve | Jesus Christ
Leadership is the skill of influencing people to work enthusiastically towards goals identified as being for the common good.
Intentions + actions = will.
Intentions - actions = squat.
Leadership begins with will.
When we serve and sacrifice for others, we build authority or influence.
Leadership is all about identifying and meeting needs.
Love is patience, kindness, humility, respectfulness, self-forgiveness, honesty, forgiveness, and commitment.
Humility isn't thinking less of yourself, but thinking of yourself less.
Active listening is attempting to see things as the speaker sees them and to feel things as the speaker feels them.
Humility is being authentic without pretense or arrogance.
The goal of any disciplinary action should be to correct or change behavior, not to punish the person.
Praise must be sincere and specific to be effective.
A good leader treats everyone as a very important person.
Respectfulness is treating others as important people.
Forgiving behavior is dealing with situations as they arise in an assertive manner and then letting go of any lingering resentment.
Honesty is being free from deception.
Honesty is about clarifying expectations, holding people accountable, and being willing to give bad news.
Honesty is being able to give people feedback, being consistent, predictable, and fair.
When we choose to love, we extend ourselves for others. This requires being patient, kind, humble, and respectful.
The committed leader is dedicated to growing, stretching, and continuously improving.
The committed leader is dedicated to becoming the best leader they can be and that the people they lead deserve.
Feelings come and go, but it is commitment that carries us through.
The true character of a leader is revealed when they have to extend themselves for tough situations.
When we serve and sacrifice, we build authority with people.
Leadership is built on authority, which is built on service, and sacrifice, which is built on love.
Love is not how you feel towards others, but how you behave towards others.
We are loving neighbors when we are as concerned about their welfare as we are concerned about our own welfare.
You can't always control the big picture, but you can control how you behave and your actions towards others.
People's feelings will follow their behavior.
Leadership is not about personality, possessions, or charisma. It is about who you are as a person.
Leadership is about substance, namely character.
Thoughts become actions, actions become habits, habits become our character, and our character becomes our destiny.
In the end, only three things matter: faith, hope, and love. The greatest of these is love.
The more I break down my pride and ego, the more joy I have in life.
The work of kindness, active listening, giving appreciation, praise, and recognition are daily missions.
Loving, serving, and extending ourselves for others forces out our self-centeredness.
Loving others pushes us forwards. Loving others forces us to grow up.
